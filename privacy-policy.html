Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain language, how P2J Industries handles the personal information you share when you use this website, request a quote or apply for a job.

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by P2J Industries ("P2J Industries", "we", "us" or "our"), a manufacturer of fabrication machinery and utility products with its works at No.834 GST Road, Keezh Edayalam, Tindivanam TK, Villupuram District, Tamil Nadu – 604302, India.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to comply with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us. This website does not require you to create an account.

  • Enquiry and quotation requests (Contact Us page): your full name, company name, phone number with country code, email address, country, state, enquiry type, subject, message, and — when you click Request a Quote on a product page — the name, model and catalogue ID of that product.
  • Job applications (Careers page): your name, email address, phone number, the position you are applying for, years of experience, a link to your resume or portfolio, and your cover note.
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.
  • WhatsApp messages: if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, the details you include in that message and your WhatsApp phone number.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, sensitive information such as bank or card details, passwords, government ID numbers or health information through this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations for products and machines;
  • process orders, deliveries, installation and after-sales support that you request;
  • assess job applications and contact candidates;
  • send product updates and offers, only if you have subscribed — you can unsubscribe at any time;
  • keep records required by law, resolve disputes and protect against fraud or misuse of the website.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Consent and Legal Basis

By submitting a form on this website or contacting us, you consent to us processing your personal data for the purpose for which you shared it, as described above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us using the details in the Contact Us section. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out, but we may no longer be able to respond to your enquiry or application.

5. How Your Information Is Shared

We share personal data only where needed to provide our services:

  • Service providers who store or deliver information for us — for example, enquiry form submissions are recorded in a Google Sheet operated through Google Workspace / Google Apps Script.
  • Messaging platforms — if you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.
  • Logistics and installation partners, only the details needed to deliver or install an order you have placed.
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law, court order or a lawful government request.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we rely on providers that apply appropriate security safeguards.

6. Cookies and Third-Party Services

This website does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies. To display the site, your browser loads some resources from third-party services, which may receive your IP address and browser information as part of that request:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces) and Google Maps (the location map on the Contact page);
  • content delivery networks such as cdnjs and jsDelivr (icons and scripts).

These services process data under their own privacy policies. If we add analytics tools in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

7. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Enquiries and quotations: for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and up to 3 years afterwards for business records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Orders and invoices: for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, so we can consider you for future openings, unless you ask us to delete your application earlier.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

8. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse — including restricting access to enquiry records to staff who need it and using reputable service providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we will act on it and notify affected people and authorities as required by law.

9. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate data;
  • ask us to erase your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Information

This website is intended for businesses and adult customers. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
