Our Story

Fabrication Discipline, Applied to Everything We Make

P2J Industries began as a fabrication shop building bending, cutting and shearing machinery for local manufacturers. That same tooling discipline — tight tolerances, powder-coated finishes, tested load ratings — now runs through everything we make, whether it's a construction-grade iron worker or a stainless kitchen basket.

We manufacture our own machines, then use them to produce our wider utility catalogue in-house. It's why a P2J wire tray holds its shape the same way a P2J shearing machine holds its tolerance.

Whether you need production machinery, custom fabrication, or household utilities, P2J Industries is a single point of contact for your full order.
